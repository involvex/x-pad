Privacy

X-Pad is a local text editor. It does not require an account and does not upload the contents of your documents to involvex or any third-party service as part of normal editing.

Preferences, recent files, session restore, and crash-recovery snapshots are stored on your machine under %APPDATA%\XPad\. Those files stay on the device unless you copy or back them up yourself.

Optional features that talk to the network:

  • GitHub Releases updater — when enabled, X-Pad may check github.com/involvex/x-pad for a newer version. Downloads start only after you confirm. Microsoft Store / MSIX installs skip this path.
  • Language servers and formatters — if you configure external tools, those programs run under your account and may follow their own privacy practices.
  • Search Web — opens your default browser with a search for the selected text; the query is handled by your browser and search provider.

Crash logs may be written locally to %APPDATA%\XPad\crash.log. They are not sent automatically.

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the X-Pad repository.
